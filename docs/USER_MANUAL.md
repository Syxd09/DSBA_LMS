# EduMetrics User Manual

## Welcome
Welcome to **EduMetrics**, either Institution's Outcome-Based Education (OBE) Management System. This manual guides you through the key features based on your role.

---

## 👨‍🎓 Student Role

**Goal**: Track academic progress and performance.

### 1. Dashboard
- **Overview**: View current SGPA, CGPA, and subject backlog.
- **Charts**: 
  - **Radar Chart**: Visualizes your strength across Bloom's Taxonomy (Apply vs. Remember).
  - **Heatmap**: Identifies weak topics vs. strong topics across all subjects.

### 2. View Results
- Navigate to **"My Results"**.
- Click on a subject to see detailed breakdown:
  - Internal marks vs. External marks.
  - Question-wise performance.
  - CO Attainment for that subject.

### 3. Download Reports
- Click the **"Download Report"** button on the dashboard top-right to get your comprehensive semester performance PDF.

---

## 👨‍🏫 Faculty Role

**Goal**: Manage courses, conduct assessments, and evaluate students.

### 1. Create an Exam
1. Go to **"Exams"** -> **"Create New Exam"**.
2. Select **Cohort** and **Subject**.
3. Choose Exam Type (Internal 1, Internal 2, etc.).
4. **Draft Question Paper**:
   - Add Questions.
   - Map each question to a **CO** (Course Outcome) and **Bloom Level**.
   - Input Max Marks.
5. Click **"Save Draft"** or **"Publish"**.

### 2. Enter Marks
1. Go to **"Marks Entry"**.
2. Select the Exam.
3. Enter marks for each student question-wise.
   - *Note: System validates that marks do not exceed question max.*
4. Click **"Submit Marks"** to send for HOD approval.

### 3. Subject Analytics
- View **"Subject Health"** to see if your class is meeting CO thresholds.
- Identify "At-Risk" students who need remedial classes.

---

## 👔 HOD (Head of Department)

**Goal**: Oversee department performance and approve academic cycles.

### 1. Approvals
- Navigate to **"Pending Approvals"**.
- Review Exam Marks submitted by Faculty.
- **Approve**: Finalizes results for students.
- **Reject**: Sends back to faculty for correction with comments.

### 2. Department Analytics
- **Faculty Comparison**: Compare CO attainment across different instructors.
- **Batch Comparison**: Track if the current batch is performing better than previous years.

### 3. Semester Promotion
- Go to **"Promotions"**.
- Review list of students eligible for next semester.
- Click **"Promote Batch"** to advance semester (e.g., Sem 3 -> Sem 4).

### 4. Reports
- Generate **NBA / NAAC Compliance Reports** (Criteria 2 & 3).
- specific PO Matrix Reports for accreditation files.

---

## 🏫 Principal Role

**Goal**: Institutional oversight and governance.

### 1. Institution Dashboard
- Monitor Health of all Departments.
- View real-time **Accreditation Readiness Score**.

### 2. Overrides
- In exceptional cases, enable **"Grade Override"** for specific students.
- *Audit Log*: All overrides are strictly logged with the reason provided.

### 3. Audit Logs
- Access **"System Logs"** to trace critical actions (who changed marks, who promoted batches).

---

## ❓ FAQ & Support

**Q: I cannot edit marks after submitting?**
A: Once submitted, marks are locked. Ask your HOD to "Reject" the submission to unlock it for editing.

**Q: My CO attainment is low?**
A: This indicates students scored poorly in questions mapped to that CO. Consider remedial classes for those specific topics.
